About

I'm a Business Analyst with 9+ years of experience across operations, supply chain, data analytics and digital projects, including hands-on work delivering health tech solutions. I help organizations turn complex business needs into practical, data-driven solutions that improve efficiency, reduce costs and support growth.

I started on the floor. I've worked in operations and supply chain roles in manufacturing and warehouse environments, from material handling and shipping and receiving to team lead and supervisor. That gave me a first-hand understanding of how processes really run: where bottlenecks form, how inventory and data accuracy affect performance, and what it takes to get teams to adopt change.

Today my work sits where people, processes and data meet. I uncover gaps, redesign workflows, define clear requirements and use data to guide better decisions. I've delivered gap analyses, business cases, requirements documents and Power BI dashboards, and worked with cross-functional teams to move projects from idea to delivery. My background in UI/UX design helps me keep the end user at the centre of every solution.

I'm open to opportunities across industries where I can help teams work smarter and deliver results that last.
